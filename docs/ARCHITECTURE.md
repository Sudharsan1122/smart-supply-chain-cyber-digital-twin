# Architecture — Smart Supply Chain Cyber Digital Twin

## Overview

The Smart Supply Chain Cyber Digital Twin is an API-first platform that
ingests telemetry and security events from a 32-asset supply chain,
maintains a live digital twin graph, detects anomalies (rule-based + ML),
correlates incidents, computes blast radius, extracts and enriches IOCs,
and produces triage verdicts.
